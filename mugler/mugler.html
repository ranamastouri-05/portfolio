ANGEL

Launched in 1992, Angel revolutionized perfumery with its gourmand notes of chocolate, vanilla and patchouli.

ALIEN

Launched in 2005, Alien is known for its mysterious character, featuring jasmine, woody notes and amber.

A*MEN

Introduced in 1996, A*Men brought a bold masculine fragrance identity with coffee, patchouli and woody notes.
